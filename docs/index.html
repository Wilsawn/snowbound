A laser scan of a Toronto street in a snowstorm, 26 Jan 2021.

In a snowstorm, how far off can a self-driving car's guess of its own position be before it can no longer find itself on its map? I tested it on this street: I pushed the car's position guess off its map on purpose and let it find its way back. Room for error is the biggest push it recovered from.

Wilsawn "Delo" Dideh, software engineer, Ottawa

How I measured it.

The map is the same loop driven in clear weather on 26 Nov 2020, two months before the storm. At 271 spots on a 20 m grid along 7.4 km of the 7.9 km loop (kept where both drives had a scan, so 20 to 40 m apart), I moved the car's position guess off the truth on purpose: 0.25 m up to 3 m, in 8 directions, with a 5 degree turn added, on both days. The matcher slid and turned the scan until it fit the map (ICP, point to plane), with its height locked to the road. A push recovered if it ended within 30 cm and 2 degrees of the truth, and a size passed when 7 of 8 directions recovered.

Room for error is the biggest push that passed before the first size that failed, counting up from the smallest; 3 m was the biggest tried, so 3 m+ means it never failed. Typical means the median. Snow cut the room at 111 spots, left it at 142 and widened it at 18.

The limits.

One drive per kind of weather. 202 no-snow spots and 120 storm spots hit the 3 m cap, so the real gap is at least what the numbers show. 12 spots fail the smallest push on both days: the method, not the snow. The last 400 m is hard on both days: the road, not the weather. Every value is good to about half a metre, the two days differ in more than snow, and a wider search window makes the numbers bigger.

What I learned: the street is still there under the snow. What the storm takes is the car's margin for being wrong, and that margin was only honest once I stopped the matcher letting the scans sink.

What's next.

More storms, the speeds the no-snow car drove at, and the wider search window. Scripts, notes and every per-spot result are at github.com/Wilsawn/snowbound.
